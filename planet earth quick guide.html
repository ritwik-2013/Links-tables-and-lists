Planet earth

planet earth quick guide

Our planet is full of incredible records, species and facts. here is a quick guide using lists

steps to protect our planet

  1. Switch of lights when you leave a room
  2. Use a reuseable water bottle every day
  3. Walk or cycle instead of travelling in a car
  4. plant a tree in your neighbourhood
  5. Spread awareness-talk to your friends

incredible natural wonders

  • Amazon rainforest - home to about 10% of all species on earth
  • great barrier reef- largest coral reef- visible from space
  • Mount everest - highest point on earth- 8849 metres
  • sahara dessert-the largest hot dessert in the world

key terms

climate change
Long term shifts in global temperature caused by human activities
biodiversity
The variety of all living species on earth
